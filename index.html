Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything parents need to know about Crystal Cliff Schools — answered clearly and honestly.

What age can my child start at Crystal Cliff Schools?
+
We accept children from as young as 2 years old into our Nursery program. Our Early Years program is designed to give toddlers a warm, play-based introduction to learning in a safe and nurturing environment.
How do I apply for admission?
+
Simply fill out the admission form on this website or visit our school in person. After submitting your application, we will contact you within 48 hours to schedule an entrance assessment and parent interview.
What is the school fee and when is it due?
+
Fees vary by class level — from ₦180,000 per term for Nursery to ₦350,000 per term for Senior Secondary. Fees are due at the beginning of each term. We offer flexible payment plans and sibling discounts of 10% from the second child.
Do you provide school bus service?
+
Yes! We operate a safe, GPS-tracked school bus service covering major routes across Lagos including Ajah, Lekki, Victoria Island, Ibeju Lekki and Sangotedo. Bus fees are charged separately per term based on your route.
What curriculum do you follow?
+
We follow the Nigerian National Curriculum approved by NERDC and WAEC. We also blend in international best practices in teaching methodology, STEM education and digital literacy to give our students a global edge.
How many students are in each class?
+
We maintain a maximum of 25 students per class to ensure every child receives personal attention from their teacher. This small class size is one of the key reasons our students consistently outperform larger schools.
Are there scholarship opportunities available?
+
Yes! We offer merit-based scholarships for exceptionally talented students in academics, sports and the arts. Scholarship applications are reviewed at the start of every academic session. Contact our admissions office for details.
Can I visit the school before applying?
+
Absolutely! We encourage all prospective parents to visit our campus. We hold Open Days every last Saturday of the month from 10AM – 2PM. You can also book a private tour by calling or WhatsApping us anytime.
What extracurricular activities are available?
+
We offer a wide range of extracurricular activities including football, basketball, swimming, music, drama, debate club, coding club, fine arts, robotics and cultural dance. Every student is encouraged to find their passion beyond the classroom.
How do you keep parents informed about their child's progress?
+
We use a dedicated Parent Portal app where you can track your child's attendance, view results, check assignments and receive real-time notifications. We also hold termly Parent-Teacher meetings and send home detailed report cards every term.
